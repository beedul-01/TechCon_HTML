Our History

TechCon is Africa's premier multi-platform technology conference that brings together thought leaders, innovators, and stakeholders from various industries, with a special focus on blockchain technology. This dynamic event explores the latest advancements in technology and their transformative impact on sectors such as government, finance, education, and business. With multiple editions catering to different audiences, TechCon is designed to foster cross-industry collaboration, spark innovation, and showcase real-world applications of emerging technologies in Africa.

,

Our Mission

TechCon's mission is to accelerate Africa's digital transformation by creating a platform where stakeholders from various industries can learn, collaborate, and innovate using emerging technologies.

Past Speakers


Dr. Kaponda

Dr. Kombe Kaponda is a seasoned specialist in financial markets infrastructure, ICT, and cybersecurity, with over 20 years of experience. A Central Banker and Chartered Digital Finance Practitioner, he holds advanced degrees in business, ICT regulation, and computer science, along with certifications from Cisco, Microsoft, and HP. His expertise spans blockchain, AI in finance, digital currencies, and regulatory compliance. He is a founding trustee of the Association for Digital Finance Practitioners in Zambia and has co-authored key publications on digital finance and cryptocurrency. Passionate about tech for development, Dr. Kaponda also enjoys farming, DIY projects, soccer, and golf.


Dalene Deale

Dalene Deale is a fintech strategist and regulatory expert who helps banks, fintechs, and regulators align compliance with commercial growth. As founder of Business Growth Hacking (Pty) Ltd, she has shaped national initiatives like SARB DFID and payment compliance programs in South Africa. Known for her clarity, creativity, and human-centered approach, Dalene transforms complex regulatory topics into strategic opportunities. Beyond her professional work, she's pursuing a doctorate in tech adoption and co-authoring a poetry book with AI — blending logic with purpose.
